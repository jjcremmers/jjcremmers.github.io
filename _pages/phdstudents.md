I lead research at the intersection of composite materials, additive manufacturing,
ceramics, and high-performance computing. My work focuses on developing advanced 
numerical models and experimental methods to understand and optimize the mechanical 
behavior of materials for demanding engineering applications. We combine 
state-of-the-art simulations using in-house developed codes, bridging the gap
between theory and practice.

In addition to research, I teach courses related to my research: 
[scientific computing](/courses/4em30/), [composite materials](/course/4mm00), and
[additive manufacturing](/courses/4mm60/)lightweight structures. My teaching 
emphasizes hands-on problem-solving, encouraging students to connect fundamental 
principles with real-world challenges.
