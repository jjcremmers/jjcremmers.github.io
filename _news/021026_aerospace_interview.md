This week, I was interviewed by Martina Silbrníková from Cursor about our new Aerospace Systems Engineering master's 
track at Eindhoven University of Technology!

The track will launch in September 2027, and applications are already open!

In the interview, we discussed how the enthusiasm of our students inspired us to develop this new track, 
how we build on the aerospace expertise already present at TU/e, and why we focus on systems engineering: 
understanding aircraft, drones and spacecraft as complex, interconnected systems.

We also talked about our ambitions to strengthen collaborations with industry in the Brainport region and 
contribute to the future of aerospace, from sustainable aviation and lightweight structures to autonomous flight.

Many thanks to Martina for the enjoyable conversation and the great article!
