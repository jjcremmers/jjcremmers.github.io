On September 25, I had the privilege of personally handing out diplomas 
to some of the graduates of our Bachelor’s programme in Mechanical Engineering 
at Eindhoven University of Technology.

It was a very special day. Having worked with many of these students in different 
courses and projects over the years, it was great to be there at the end of their 
Bachelor’s and celebrate this moment with them and their families.

To all our graduates: congratulations on this achievement! I wish you all the 
best in your future studies and careers. And I am very happy to have already seen 
many familiar faces continuing their journey in our Master’s programme in 
Mechanical Engineering.





📷 Levi Baruch, Bart van Overbeeke
