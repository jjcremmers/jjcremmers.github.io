I have been interview by Cursor on my work onthe use of ChatGPT in education.

Does ChatGPT make you smarter? Or lazier and more dependent?
More and more students are using ChatGPT for their studies, 
but does it actually help them move forward?

ChatGPT and other generative AI models can be useful tools for students. 
Nonetheless, their use also raises questions about dependence, critical thinking, 
creativity and the line between original work and plagiarism. Associate professor 
Joris Remmers explores the pros and cons of this technology in education.
