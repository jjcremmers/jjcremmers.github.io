We are excited to announce that registration is now open for the new Aerospace Systems Engineering track at 
Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)! The track is part of the Master's programme in Mechanical 
Engineering and will welcome its first students in September 2027.

The track offers a multidisciplinary approach to aerospace engineering, combining aerodynamics, structures and 
materials, propulsion, and control systems. With a strong focus on systems engineering, students will learn to 
design, analyse, and integrate complex aerospace systems. Through hands-on projects and close connections with 
the aerospace industry, we aim to prepare the next generation of aerospace engineers.

We look forward to welcoming our first group of students in September 2027 and building this exciting new 
programme together!

Interested in joining us? Discover the programme and admission information on the TU/e [Aerospace Systems
Engineering website](https://www.tue.nl/en/education/graduate-school/master-mechanical-engineering/masters-track-aerospace-systems-engineering).
